# Mood notes

How mood words map onto measurable song features in this catalog.

## Chill, relaxed and calm

Low energy, high acousticness or high instrumentalness, mid-to-low tempo. "Chill"
in this catalog is a mood value in its own right, but the measurable signature
matters more than the label.

- hint: energy = 0.25
- hint: acousticness = 0.6

## Moody, melancholic and sad

Low valence with mid energy. Moody is not the same as low energy: several moody
tracks here are mid-tempo and fairly dense.

- hint: energy = 0.5
- hint: mode = vibe_and_energy

## Happy, upbeat and euphoric

High valence and high danceability. Euphoric is the mood tag that most often
accompanies these.

- hint: energy = 0.8
- hint: danceability = 0.8
- hint: detailed_mood_tag = euphoric

## Intense and aggressive

High energy with low acousticness. Tempo is less reliable here than energy: some
aggressive tracks are mid-tempo but very dense.

- hint: energy = 0.9
- hint: acousticness = 0.15

## Romantic

Mid-low energy, high valence, vocal-led. Almost always r&b in this catalog.

- hint: energy = 0.45
- hint: genre = r&b
